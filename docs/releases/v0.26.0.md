# World of ClaudeCraft（克劳德争霸） v0.26.0 Release Notes
**Release:** v0.26.0
**Date:** 2026-07-15
**Previous release:** v0.25.0
v0.26.0 is a dungeon finder, itemization, combat, social, and production hardening release, with 131 commits since v0.25.0. It adds the Dungeon Finder with auto-queue and premade boards, a Hit Rating stat with a complete heroic itemization pass, three tank defensive cooldowns, a signed Windows desktop installer, node harvesting you can actually click, weapon sheathing, production stability hardening with realm admission caps and lease takeover, and 11 GLB prop replacements across the Drowned Litany and related zones.
## Highlights
- **Dungeon Finder:** a new Shift+I window with activity catalogue, Quick Match role queue, and Premade Board covers all four dungeons, their heroic variants, and the 奈斯拉西斯（Nythraxis） raid in both difficulties. Matches form automatically via deterministic FIFO and never touch the sim's RNG ([#1789](https://github.com/levy-street/world-of-claudecraft/pull/1789)).
- **Hit Rating and heroic itemization:** a new Hit Rating stat (10 rating = 1% hit) caps the above-level miss/resist penalty at roughly 26%/25% for Heroic (+3 level), down from roughly 44%/43%. Every ilvl-31 heroic boss-set piece and every 奈斯拉西斯（Nythraxis） raid piece gains one or two combat ratings, and Heroic Quartermaster rings and amulets each carry 2.5% in one combat rating chosen by archetype. PvP gear is rebalanced so it is no longer a PvE stat upgrade over same-tier dungeon gear ([#1860](https://github.com/levy-street/world-of-claudecraft/pull/1860)).
- **Tank defensive cooldowns:** 战士s get Ironhold (40% damage reduction, 8 sec), 圣骑士s get Sacred Bulwark (a guardian ward that denies a killing blow and restores 35% HP), and 德鲁伊s get Primal Reflexes (50% dodge, 60 sec cooldown). 德鲁伊 bear parity is also fixed ([#1912](https://github.com/levy-street/world-of-claudecraft/pull/1912)).
- **Windows desktop client:** the signed Windows installer is now live. The "Download for Windows" button appears on the website and play page, and the installer is Authenticode-signed via Azure Key Vault, removing the unsigned-publisher warning (SmartScreen reputation for the new signing identity builds over the first weeks of installs) ([#1961](https://github.com/levy-street/world-of-claudecraft/pull/1961), [#1944](https://github.com/levy-street/world-of-claudecraft/pull/1944)).
