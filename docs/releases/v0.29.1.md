## 更新内容说明

版本 v0.29.1 发布，包含游戏底层架构迭代与前期核心系统打磨。

A balance hotfix for the v0.29.0 heroic retune: healers can keep up again, tanks stop dying to dice rolls, and the fights that overshot got retuned.
## Healers
- Every healing spell gains a new or stronger top rank at level 20 (leveling ranks unchanged).
- Healing now benefits from double Spell Power: every point of Intellect adds 1 healing.
## Tanks
- Monsters can no longer critically strike committed tanks: Protection warriors, Protection paladins, and Feral druids in Sloth Form.
- Protection paladin and Sloth Form durability raised to stand alongside Protection warriors.
## 奈斯拉西斯（Nythraxis） (raid)
- Gravebreaker is now a charged attack released on his next landed swing: no more instant opener, and it can no longer stack with a melee hit. Dodging his swing delays it.
- Heroic: 奈斯拉西斯（Nythraxis） swings for less, his skeleton waves have far less health, and all court adds hit much less hard. Voss can now realistically be slowed and kited.
