# World of ClaudeCraft（克劳德争霸） v0.25.0 Release Notes
**Release:** v0.25.0
**Date:** 2026-07-14
**Previous release:** v0.24.2
v0.25.0 is a progression, endgame, PvP, and presentation release, with 350 commits since v0.24.2. It adds the Book of Deeds achievement system, Heroic 奈斯拉西斯（Nythraxis）, real signature abilities and identity masteries for all 27 specializations, Honor and Warfare PvP progression, the 克劳德币（Claudium） storefront and Season 1 Armory cosmetics, a production sound workflow, richer world models and bag art, player mute and identity tools, a rebuilt online-only /play entry, and a broad reliability and operations pass.
## Highlights
- **The Book of Deeds:** 192 achievements worth 2,365 Renown across progression, combat, dungeons, delves, Chronicles, collection, PvP, social play, exploration, and hidden feats. Rewards are cosmetic: 19 titles, three character-sheet badge borders, and public rarity and Renown standings ([#1785](https://github.com/levy-street/world-of-claudecraft/pull/1785)).
- **Heroic 奈斯拉西斯（Nythraxis）:** the 10-player raid gains a heroic tier with the Deathless Court encounter, interruptible Malric's Mending, raid-tier heroic set and legendary upgrades, and three heroic-only weapons ([#1816](https://github.com/levy-street/world-of-claudecraft/pull/1816)).
- **Talents 2.0, specialization update:** selecting any of the 27 specializations now grants a real signature ability and a level-scaled identity mastery. This ships the specialization foundation and 24 new ability definitions without the later choice-row phase ([#1543](https://github.com/levy-street/world-of-claudecraft/pull/1543)).
- **Honor and Warfare:** ranked Arena wins, Fiesta combat, and results now award persistent Honor. FURY, the Honor Quartermaster, sells 40 item-level 28 epic PvP pieces, and the new Warfare rating affects player-versus-player damage only ([#1817](https://github.com/levy-street/world-of-claudecraft/pull/1817)).
