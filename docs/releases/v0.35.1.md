# World of ClaudeCraft（克劳德争霸） v0.35.1 Release Notes
**Release:** v0.35.1
**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Previous release:** v0.35.0
Version 0.35.1 is a performance patch on the 0.35 line, on both sides of the wire. On the
server, the two heaviest per-tick snapshot reads (mail and the commission order board) are
now gated on change and cadence, and Ravenpost mail lookups stop scanning the whole book.
In the renderer, the spell VFX stack sheds its per-frame allocation and upload costs and
weapon skins fade with distance instead of ticking forever. The patch also pins a
dependency security advisory and ships fully localized across all 21 locales.
