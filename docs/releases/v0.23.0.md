# World of ClaudeCraft（克劳德争霸） - v0.23.0 Release Notes
**Release:** v0.23.0
**Date:** 2026-07-09
**Previous release:** v0.22.0
v0.23.0 is the biggest content release to date, with **680 commits** since v0.22.0 (this range also folds in the unpublished v0.22.1 hotfix line). It ships two brand-new game modes (the Vale Cup boarball tournament and the Protect Yumi PvP maze), heroic difficulty for all four five-player dungeons with a new Heroic Mark currency, the professions endgame (archetype-gated crafting power, specialization perks, town focus, signed materials), the personal bank vault, a major itemization wave (Crit/Haste Rating, legendary and epic-set procs), whole-group percent raid buffs, fluid hit-and-run melee for every mob, display-side movement prediction that makes online play feel like offline, a first-class mobile touch HUD, group-visible loot rolls, an accuracy audit of all 157 ability tooltips, Czech as the 22nd supported locale, and a production observability rollout (Prometheus game metrics plus an on-demand admin tick profiler).
## Highlights
- **The Vale Cup:** a full boarball (football) minigame at the Sowfield stadium in 东溪谷（Eastbrook） Vale: 1v1 to 5v5 queues, sport role kits, bot backfill, spectator parimutuel betting, player and guild leaderboards, and daily-reward hooks (#1586).
- **Heroic dungeons:** all four five-player dungeons gain a heroic difficulty with retuned level-22 mobs, plus personal Heroic Mark drops spent at the new Heroic Quartermaster in Highwatch, the game's only source of neck and ring jewelry (#1575, #1693).
- **Professions completed:** the active archetype now gates crafting power (two uncapped majors, a rare-capped hobby, common caps elsewhere), with specialization perks, combo recipes, signed materials, Battlefield Experience, town focus allocation, archetype titles, and the level-20 crafting hub at Highwatch (#1638 and the #1207 to #1281 series).
- **The Gilded Strongbox:** a personal bank vault at bursar NPCs in all three hub towns: 24 base slots, purchasable expansions to 96, entitlement bonus slots to an absolute 112, an append-only audit ledger, and an operator audit script (#1630).
